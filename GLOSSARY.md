# Yway

Yway lets young people explore careers through realistic simulated work and choose a reversible direction toward deeper learning and work.

## Language

**Career**:
The type of work being explored. A Career may have multiple distinct Simulations over time.

**Tracer Career**:
A Career selected to validate Yway's Career Simulation experience contract in a representative work context. Selection does not imply public-launch selection, priority over other Careers, or a template that every Career must copy.

**Validation Cohort**:
The group selected to experience a Simulation for qualitative validation in a stated context, distinct from people contributing separate workplace realism input. It does not define Yway's final launch audience.

**Simulation**:
A bounded exploration experience within a Career, containing Work Episodes and multiple Missions in total.

**Simulation Completion**:
Reaching the authored overall ending of a bounded Simulation after completing the core Work Episodes required on the user's actual route, including through Guided Fallback. It does not require alternative branches, optional extensions, replay, Reflection answers, or a next-direction commitment, and does not imply comprehensive Career experience or capability.

**Exploration Stopped Early**:
A valid exploration outcome in which the user intentionally leaves before the authored Simulation ending after experiencing part of it. Only the Missions and Work Episodes actually experienced are acknowledged; the whole Simulation remains unfinished, without implying failure.

**Work Episode**:
A connected work situation within a Simulation containing one or more Missions, with a coherent user role and relevant workplace context, user work, and consequences carried forward whenever they matter. A new Work Episode starts with fresh work context by default; any intentional continuation from an earlier Episode is made explicit to the user.

**Work Episode Completion**:
Reaching a meaningful ending of the authored route through a connected work situation, with any unresolved or pending matters made understandable. Different branches may end with different legitimate outcomes; completion does not require visiting every branch or resolving every outstanding business problem.

**Mission**:
A short, approximately 5–10 minute slice of meaningful work within a Work Episode. Tools and repetitive mechanics may be simplified while preserving the important judgment and work interaction.

**Mission Completion**:
Reaching a Mission's meaningful work stopping point through the user's Committed Action or explicitly chosen Guided Fallback, with the relevant Consequence or immediate changed situation presented. It is completion of the exploration experience, distinct from independently performing the substantive work, and does not require consuming coaching or answering Reflection prompts.

**Preparation**:
The user's inspection, drafting, revision, and optional help before committing a work decision.

**Committed Action**:
The workplace action that commits a work decision and causes its relevant Consequence, such as sending a response or handing over work. Preparation is distinct from a Committed Action.

**Consequence**:
The changed simulated work situation resulting from a Committed Action. It may include an immediate change with an uncertain or delayed outcome, revealed later in a connected Mission or through an explicit authored time jump.

**Qualitative Coaching**:
Concise contextual feedback about the user's work, its consequences, and relevant trade-offs, without scores or capability or career-fit verdicts.

**Guided Fallback**:
An explicitly chosen demonstration or heavily scaffolded handling of the current work that lets a stuck user continue experiencing the Career and its consequences. It is guided or observed work, distinct from independently completed user work, and is not verified evidence of capability.

**Independent Work**:
The substantive work the user performs themselves, distinct from a demonstrated or heavily scaffolded portion supplied through Guided Fallback. Later independently performed actions remain the user's own work even after Guided Fallback; independence alone does not imply verified capability or verified evidence.

**Input Support**:
Assistance that helps the user enter their own intended response while leaving substantive wording and work judgment with them. It is distinct from Guided Fallback, which demonstrates, supplies, or heavily scaffolds substantive work.

**Reflection**:
The user's consideration of specific work moments, their reactions, and their preferences, primarily for self-understanding. It supports a reversible next direction without a scored assessment or career-fit verdict.
